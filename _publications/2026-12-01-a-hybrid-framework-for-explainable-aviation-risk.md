Extracts causal chains from aviation safety reports and grounds them with physics-based failure quantification and interventional analysis, producing risk explanations an operator can check.

Code, extracted chains, learned networks, and judge outputs are archived on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23934456), with a mirror on [GitHub](https://github.com/YutianPangASU/AviationSafetyReportKnowledgeGraph).
